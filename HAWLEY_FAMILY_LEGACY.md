### **1. Core Lineage & Family Members**

* **Patriarch Samuel Emanuel Hawley ("Manny") (1929–2018):**
  * **Lineage:** Son of William Hawley and Phyllis Ann Wattley.
  * **Community & Residence:** Long-time resident of Estate Mon Bijou, St. Croix.
  * **Vital Record:** Passed away on January 16, 2018, at age 88.

* **Wayne Samuel Hawley Sr. ("Snoop") (1970 – December 11, 2011):**
  * **Parents:** Son of Samuel Emanuel Hawley and Sarah Florence Greenaway.
  * **Community & Residence:** Lifelong resident of Estate Rattan, St. Croix.
  * **Workforce & Social Ties:** Maintained strong ties within St. Croix's industrial workforce, notably among colleagues and friends at the **Hovensa oil refinery**.
  * **Vital Record:** Passed away on December 11, 2011, at age 41 (*VI Source Archive*, Dec 20, 2011).
  * **Services & Interment:** Viewing at 9:00 a.m., funeral service at 10:00 a.m. on Friday, December 23, 2011, at **Bethel Methodist Church**; attendees were requested to wear festive colors. Interment followed at **Kingshill Cemetery**. Funeral arrangements entrusted to **James Memorial Funeral Home Inc.**

* **Wayne Samuel Hawley Jr. (b. July 9, 1992):**
  * **Lineage:** Son of Wayne Samuel Hawley Sr. and grandson of Samuel Emanuel Hawley.
  * **Athletics & Early Youth:** Competed in Junior Varsity cross country for the St. Croix Track Club (VIPace) in 2007.
  * **Direct Generational Successor:** Alongside his sister Aaliyah, represents the direct succeeding generation on St. Croix.

* **Aaliyah Hawley:**
  * **Lineage:** Daughter of Wayne Samuel Hawley Sr. and sister of Wayne Jr., carrying forward the Crucian Hawley family lineage.

### **3. Youth Mentorship & Music Career**

* **Secondary School Preparation (St. Croix Central High School):**
  * Wayne Jr. developed as a pianist and composer at Central High under Band Director **Stan Joines**.
  * Commended alongside his classmate by Central High Principal **Chermaine Johnson** as *"brilliant and extremely talented young men always willing to share their abilities with others."*
* **Berklee College of Music Matriculation:**
  * Wayne Jr. entered the **Berklee College of Music** in Boston, Massachusetts, for the Spring 2011 semester, majoring in music composition.
  * Matriculated alongside fellow Central High graduate and trumpeter **Ernel Edmond**, who received a $7,000 scholarship from the same fund to study trumpet performance and composition.
* **Stanley & the Ten Sleepless Knights Scholarship (August 2010):**
  * Awarded a **$3,000 higher education scholarship** presented by legendary quelbe band leader **Stanley Jacobs** during an official ceremony hosted at the **WTJX-Channel 12 Studio in Estate Richmond**.
* **EDC Corporate Co-Sponsorship:**
  * The award was co-funded by **Professional Holding Co.** (represented by Vice President **Rob Wunschel**), an Economic Development Commission (EDC) beneficiary company fulfilling its statutory mandate to reinvest tax-incentivized funds into local Virgin Islands arts and youth education programs.
* **Essential Community Support Network:**
  * This community and institutional backing provided vital stability, enabling Wayne Jr. to maintain his university studies uninterrupted during a time of profound personal grief following his father's untimely death in December 2011.
